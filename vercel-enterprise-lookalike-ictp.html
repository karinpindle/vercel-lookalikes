Vercel Enterprise Customer Lookalikes

A lookalike scoring model built from ~5,000 confirmed Vercel enterprise accounts through HG's technographic tracking. This was done until Vercel shares their Enterprise customer list with HG. After that, the scoring criteria will be re-assessed and updated and new sample accounts will be identified and scored.

We scored every company in the HG database on firmographic similarity (scale, industry, IT spend) and technographic alignment (modern web stack, cloud-native infrastructure, headless architecture) to surface whitespace prospects that mirror the known enterprise base.

How to read the scored columns
F · N · I0–100 each

The three composite scores behind every account: Fit (firmographic + budget similarity to the seed customers), Need (technographic + AI-stack alignment), and Intent (active research signals). They combine 55 / 30 / 15 into the final 0–100 score and tier.

Dev Spend %max 15 pts

A sub-score inside Fit. Measures the share of the account's IT budget spent on application development + cloud (PaaS / IaaS / cloud services). Higher share = more digitally-led = warmer. Scored: 0 (<10% or no data) → 7 (10–25%) → 15 (25–45%, ideal) → 12 (45%+, likely a software firm itself). Maximum 15.

GenAImax 15 pts

The GenAI Maturity sub-score inside Need. Measures the breadth of the account's adopted generative-AI stack — foundation-model APIs (OpenAI, Anthropic, Bedrock, Vertex…) plus AI application infrastructure (Pinecone, LangChain, Hugging Face, Databricks…). More of the stack adopted = more mature = warmer, since AI-building companies need fast frontends. Maximum 15.

Enable Sales · More use cases›

What else sellers can do with HG data. Beyond the pre-meeting brief, HG-powered context unlocks conversational, in-the-flow workflows that sellers actually use day-to-day.

Worked example · Pre-meeting brief Netflix Account Brief → A seller-ready brief generated from this ICTP — score, opportunities, spend, stack, buying centers, contacts, and outreach. Open brief ↗
"Why this account?" on demand
Seller asks the agent why an account is prioritized — Sales Coach replies with the specific HG signals (intent surge, tech install, cloud-spend trend, leadership change, contract-renewal window) that pushed it up the list, with sources.
In-context coaching
Agent suggests discovery questions, objection handling, and value framing tied to the account's actual stack, industry pains, and recent moves — not generic talk tracks.
Tailored outreach generation
One click drafts personalized emails, LinkedIn messages, and call openers grounded in HG firmographic, technographic, and intent data so every touch references something real and recent.
Trigger outreach sequences
Push HG-enriched contacts straight into Outreach, Salesloft, or your sequencer of choice — with the right persona, role, and signal context already mapped to the cadence.
Contact discovery & enrichmentNew HG offering
Find the right buying-committee members via HG contact_search and contact_enrich — email, phone, title, and recent job changes — without leaving the agent.
Account plan refresh
Re-run the brief on a cadence (weekly, pre-QBR, post-earnings) to surface what changed: new intent topics, tech adds/drops, leadership moves, fresh filings — keeping account plans alive instead of static.
Whitespace & expansion signals
For existing customers, surface adjacent products, new sites, M&A activity, and tech gaps that map to Vercel's portfolio — feeding CS and AE expansion plays.
Real-time competitive intel
Detect competitor installs, displacement signals, and renewal windows so sellers walk in knowing exactly where they stand against incumbents.
